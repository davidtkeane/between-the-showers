# Guinea pig temperature — where these numbers come from

Not invented. Sourced 2026-09-12, because this is the one project on the list where
being wrong has a cost.

| °C | state | what it means |
|----|-------|---------------|
| **below 15** | 🔴 **DANGER COLD** | RSPCA: **move them indoors** |
| 15–17 | 🟡 chilly | watch it, especially overnight |
| **17–23** | 🟢 **IDEAL** | RSPCA gives 17–20 as ideal; other sources 18–23 |
| 23–26 | 🟡 warm | hyperthermia possible from **24** in susceptible animals |
| **above 26** | 🔴 **DANGER HOT** | heatstroke risk; most common above 28 |

**Guinea pigs cannot sweat.** Heatstroke onset is rapid and can be fatal.

**Warning signs:** panting, drooling, lying flat, weakness, refusing food.

**RSPCA on outdoor hutches:** shelter from direct sun *and* direct wind. Below 15 °C,
bring them in.

### ⚠️ Phase 1 caveat — read this
Phase 1 uses the **outdoor air temperature for Dublin 16**, not the temperature inside
the hutch. A hutch in direct sun can be **10 °C or more hotter** than the air around it;
a sheltered one at night can be a few degrees warmer. So treat phase 1 as an **early
warning that conditions are heading the wrong way**, not as a measurement of where they
actually live.

Phase 2 (a €4 DS18B20 in the hutch) is what turns this from a forecast into a fact.
